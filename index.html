How TechYard works

TechYard is the parent company. Each product has its own brand, its own customers and its own team focus, while sharing tools, know-how and a single standard of work.

We start small, launch fast and improve with real feedback from the people who use what we build.

What every business shares

  • ToolsEach team starts from the same set, not from zero.
  • Know-howWhat one product learns, the others get.
  • One standard of workThe same bar for quality across every brand.

How each product gets built

  1. Start smallBuild the smallest version that is useful.
  2. Launch fastPut it in front of real people early.
  3. Improve with feedbackLet what customers say decide what comes next.

Then we go around again.
